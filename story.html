Our Story

Purnanna was born from a simple idea — that the taste of home should not have to be left behind.

For many of us living away from Maharashtra, finding authentic homemade staples and familiar Marathi brand products often means juggling multiple stores, long drives, or going without.

Purnanna started as a way to bring everything together in one place. We wanted to make it easy for families in Australia to access freshly made chutneys, traditional snacks, and the trusted pantry brands they grew up with — without the hassle.

Based in Melbourne, we prepare our homemade items fresh and source our branded products from reliable Marathi suppliers. Every order is packed with care and delivered Australia-wide.

Today, Purnanna serves a growing community of families who share a love for authentic Maharashtrian food. We continue to expand our range, guided by the flavours and traditions that matter most to our customers.
